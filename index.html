Put it on your home screen (iPhone)
  1. Tap Share at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Tap the new Pit Wall icon to play.
On Android
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome and choose Add to Home screen.
